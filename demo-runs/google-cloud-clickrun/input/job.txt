Software Engineer III — Cloud Infrastructure
Crescent Labs

We're hiring a Software Engineer III for our Cloud Infrastructure team. You
will work on the foundational primitives that other engineering teams build on
— compute scheduling, multi-tenant networking, and storage abstractions.

What you'll do:
- Design and build core infrastructure services in Go and Python.
- Improve the reliability of multi-tenant resource allocation under load.
- Operate production services with strong observability and on-call practices.
- Partner with security and SRE teams on incident response and post-mortems.

What we look for:
- 4+ years of backend or systems experience.
- Strong fundamentals in distributed systems.
- Experience operating production services, including on-call.
- Comfort with Linux, Kubernetes, and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform).
- Bonus: prior work on storage internals or networking.

Tech: Go, Python, Kubernetes, Terraform, PostgreSQL, etcd, gRPC, Linux.
