Senior Backend Engineer — Payments Platform

We're hiring a Senior Backend Engineer for our Payments Platform team. You'll
own the services that move money through our product, working closely with
product, fraud, and compliance teams.

What you'll do:
- Design, build, and operate high-throughput payment services in Python.
- Improve the reliability and observability of our billing and invoicing pipeline.
- Lead the design of new subsystems and review the work of more junior engineers.
- Partner with our fraud team to ship safer money-movement workflows.
- Drive incremental migrations from our legacy monolith to focused services.

What we're looking for:
- 4+ years of backend experience, with significant time in Python.
- Experience operating production services with on-call responsibilities.
- Comfortable with PostgreSQL, queueing systems, and event-driven architectures.
- A track record of mentoring teammates and raising the bar on code quality.
- Bonus: prior experience with billing, payments, or any regulated domain.

Tech you'll touch: Python, PostgreSQL, Kafka, Kubernetes, AWS, Datadog.
